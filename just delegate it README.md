# Just Delegate It

Source: *Just Delegate It: A Crash Course* (Panaversity Agent Factory): https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/just-delegate-it-crash-course. 

## 1. Course ka maqsad

**Ek line mein:** AI se sawal poochna nahi, usay **kaam** dena, aur phir result ko khud check karke accept karna.

**Kyun zaroori hai:** AI ghanton ka kaam minuton mein kar deta hai, magar wo ye nahi bata sakta ke uske result par bharosa karna theek hai ya nahi. Wo faisla hamesha aap ka rehta hai.

**Course ka opener (experiment):** AI se 3 free courses ki table banwao, phir ek link kholo. Teen cheezein ho sakti hain: page table se mel khata hai; page kisi detail (price, language) par alag hai; ya link kisi course tak jata hi nahi. AI ne kaam kar diya, magar sachchai ka saboot aap ko khud dhoondna parta hai.

**Course ka order:** pehle job, phir machine, phir collaboration, phir techniques. Is course mein sirf **job dena aur result accept karna** hai. Sab concepts ek hi running example par chalte hain: free courses ki table.

## 2. Delegation Loop (course ki reedh ki haddi)

| Step | Matlab | Kaun |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Define** | kya banna chahiye | aap |
| **Delegate** | zimmedari AI ko dena | aap |
| **Observe** | AI kaam kare, aap dekhein | AI kaam, aap nigrani |
| **Intervene** | bhatke to aap rokein aur theek karein | aap |
| **Verify** | zaroori claims check karein | aap |
| **Accept** | use karein ya wapas bhejein | aap |

**Course ka usool:** AI beech ka malik hai, aap dono siron ke. Beech mein AI kaam karta hai magar nigrani aap ki rehti hai. **Intervene insaan karta hai**, AI nahi.

**Do wapsi arrows:** Intervene se Delegate (redirect), aur Accept se Define (send it back).

**Delegation Loop kya hai aur kya nahi:** ye ek insaan ka ek kaam chalane ka rhythm hai. AI Fluency ka Delegation (D1) wo faisla hai ke kaun sa kaam kis ka. Delegation Map (Workflow Design course) us faisle ka likha hua roop hai. Loop us kaam ko chalata hai jo AI ko diya gaya.

## 3. Part 1: Job kaise dena hai

### Concept 1: Asking vs Giving the Job

**Easy:** "AI seekhne ke achhe courses kaun se hain?" poochhne se list milti hai aur agla saara kaam aap ka rehta hai. "3 free courses ki table banao jis mein provider, language, waqt aur link ho" kehne se aap ke paas check karne ko ek **cheez** hoti hai.

**Technical:** farq jumle ki lambai ka nahi. Job "ek cheez ke wujood mein aane" ko kehti hai (table, document). "Delegate" ka matlab result banane ki zimmedari AI ko dena hai. Is ke liye paid plan ya khaas model nahi chahiye, sirf jumle ki qism badalti hai.

**Verify:** table se ek link kholo. Page mel khata hai to theek, warna table sirf dekhne mein achhi thi.

**Agar ye na ho:** aap AI se sirf advice lete hain aur agla poora kaam khud karte hain, ya AI ka jawab bina check ke maan lete hain.

**Exam trap:** "usne list li, phir khud compare kiya, kaun sa step delegate ho sakta tha?" Jawab: comparison. Verify aur final decision aap ke paas rehte hain.

### Concept 2: Outcome, not clicks

**Easy:** recipe ("sirf in do sites par jao, top 3 lo") mein aap ka plan hi hadd ban jata hai. Natija ("jab ye khatam ho to mere paas ye cheez honi chahiye") mein AI tareeqa chunta hai aur aap result ko apne maange hue natije se judge karte hain.

**Technical:** steps **tab** likho jab source ya order lazmi ho (wo constraint hai). Steps **ghabrahat mein** likhna galat hai. Course ka usool: ghabrahat ka ilaaj shuru mein steps likhna nahi, **aakhir mein check karna** hai. Chhupa khatra: "highest-rated" jaise lafz AI khud bana sakta hai agar page par rating ho hi na.

**Agar ye na ho:** agar behtareen cheez aap ki di hui sites par nahi, to table saaf hogi magar recommendation kamzor, aur aap ko pata nahi chalega.

**Exam trap:** recipe wale sawal mein sahi jawab: "diye gaye sites ke bahar behtar cheez ho sakti thi, aur result mein koi nishani nahi." Ghalat: "AI calculation nahi kar sakta", "chart chahiye tha."

### Concept 3: Delegation Brief (kaam ka parcha)

| Line | Sawal |
| --- | --- |
| **Outcome** | kya wujood mein aana chahiye? |
| **Context** | AI jo khud nahi jaan sakta (source bhi) |
| **Constraints** | shartein aur hadein |
| **Authority** | AI akele kya tay kare, kis par poochhe? |
| **Deliverable** | kis shakal mein (table, file) |
| **Verification** | accept se pehle kya check, **kaam shuru hone se pehle** likho |

**Technical:**

- **Authority** chat mein bhi hoti hai jahan AI kuch bhej nahi sakta. "Barabar ho to chhota chun lo" authority hai; "paid nikle to mujhe batao" authority par limit hai. Is line ke bina AI chupke se faisle kar leta hai.
- **Verification** pehle likhne se result "parhna" nahi, "check karna" ban jata hai.
- Brief ek soch ka tool hai, form nahi. Do line ke kaam ke liye 6 boxes nahi bharte. Magar jab bhi result se mayoosi ho, 6 mein se ek sawal unanswered tha.
- Agar koi line is kaam par lagti hi nahi, to "none" likho, delete mat karo, taake dikhe ke aap ne faisla kiya.
- Paste karne se pehle naam aur private cheezein hata do.

**Authority aur Performance mein farq:** Authority = AI ke **faislon ki hadd** (is ek job ke liye). Performance (AI Fluency ka Description) = AI ka **mizaj** (challenge, uncertainty, politeness).

**Brief ki lines aur 4Ds (meri mapping):** Outcome (Delegation + Description), Context (Description), Constraints (Description + Diligence), Authority (Delegation + Diligence), Deliverable (Description), Verification (Discernment + Diligence).

**Agar ye na ho:** result generic ho jata hai, ya AI chupke se ghalat faisle karta hai, ya aap ke paas result judge karne ka maiyaar hi nahi hota.

**Exam trap:** brief mein outcome, context, constraints aur deliverable the, magar result ne bina bataye paid course recommend kar diya. Jawab: **Authority** line. Practice exam: hospital volunteer letter (Q49) mein context khali thi; bakery founder ke "better reporting" (Q35) mein outcome, reader, frequency, data aur format khali the. Dono mein masla AI ka nahi, ek khali line ka tha.

## 4. Part 2: Control tab seekho jab job ko zaroorat ho

**Usool:** need first, then tool.

### Concept 4: AI ko research dena

**Easy:** AI do tareeqon se jaanta hai: **yaad se** (training se, bara magar purana) aur **abhi dekh kar** (search, current aur source ke saath). Price, date, "course abhi chal raha hai" jaisi badalne wali cheezon ke liye sirf search chalta hai.

**Technical:**

- Jo fact dekh kar aaya, uske saath ek **source** hona chahiye jise aap khol sakein. Jo yaad se aaya wo akela aata hai aur utna hi pakka lagta hai.
- Agar current claim bina source ke aaye, to ye na maano ke AI ne dekha. AI ka apne baare mein bayan saboot nahi.
- **Source tareekh nahi hota.** Jab "current" zaroori ho, page par date khud dhoondo. Live cheezon (rate, score) mein waqt bhi dekho.
- Brief mein: "har course ke liye batao ke search se mila ya pehle se pata tha, aur jo page use kiya uski date do." "Searched" aur "knew" ishara hain, saboot source aur date hain.
- **Deep research mode** tab jab deliverable hi report ho. Ek fact ke liye ek search kaafi hai.

**Verify:** ek fact chuno jo page ke saath aaya, page kholo, support karne wala jumla dhoondo, date dekho. Jumla nahi mila ya date purani hai to link sirf sajawat tha.

> **Citation verification nahi hai. Citation ko check karna verification hai.**

**Agar ye na ho:** AI purani ya invented cheez current bana kar de deta hai.

**Exam trap:** AI ne teen courses ki confident description di, na link na date. Jawab: shayad yaad se jawab diya, ye na samjho ke usne dekha. Practice exam: water utility (Q51), regulator ka charge ek page par hai aur board us par faisla karega, to web search se wo page nikalo aur link rakho.

### Concept 5: AI ko apna source dena

**Easy:** kuch jobs ko wo cheez chahiye jo internet par nahi: aap ka syllabus, office ka notice, message thread. Aap usay khud dete hain.

**Do jadoo wali lines:**

1. "Using only the text below" (sirf is text se)
2. "Quote the sentence you used" (jo jumla use kiya wo quote karo)

Inke bina AI aap ke text ko apni yaad ke saath mila deta hai aur aap farq nahi kar sakte. In ke saath har jawab ek jumle ki taraf ishara karta hai, aur **"not in the source"** ek achha jawab ban jata hai, wo gap nahi jise AI khud bhar de.

**Technical:**

- Chhota text paste karo; lamba document attach karo.
- **Pehle private cheezein nikalo** (naam, phone, account numbers, wo sab jo public notice board par na lagate). Free assistant aap ki filing cabinet nahi.
- **Temporary ya Incognito chat** history aur memory mein save nahi hoti, **lekin paste kiya hua text gayab nahi hota**, provider ek muddat tak copy rakhta hai. Isliye pehla usool pehle lagta hai.
- Lambi chat kharab ho to bahas mat karo: AI se summary likhwao (kya tay hua, kya baaqi), copy karo, nayi chat kholo aur summary pehla message banao. **Summary bhi ek source hai.**

**Verify:** ek quoted jumla asal text mein dhoondo. Lafz ba lafz mila: theek. Qareeb magar hubahu nahi: AI ne paraphrase kiya aur "quote" naam de diya. Bilkul nahi mila: AI ne invent kiya.

**Boundary (meri wazahat):** boundary **zaroorat** se lagao ("sirf school ki approved website se", "sirf is document se"), **dar** se nahi.

**Agar ye na ho:** AI aap ke source ko apni yaad se mila kar aisi baatein likh deta hai jo source mein hain hi nahi.

**Exam trap:** notice paste karne ke baad ek jawab mein aisi deadline thi jo notice mein nahi thi. Jawab: brief mein "use only the text below" aur "quote the sentence" nahi thi. Practice exam: toy company (Q41), deal ki terms hain, koi regulated data nahi, workspace internal confidential ke liye approved hai, aur chat history ya Memory mein nahi rakhna, to **Incognito chat**, magar company ki **retention policy phir bhi lagu hoti hai.** Wedding hall (Q10) ka sawal bhi isi concept ka hai: summary mein do grounds teeno letters mein nahi, to dobara run karo, sirf teen letters tak limit karke, har point ka jumla quote karwa kar.

### Concept 6: Deliverable maango, jawab nahi

**Easy:** **Answer** chat mein jawab hai. **Deliverable** wo cheez hai jo save ho sake aur aage di ja sake (table, document, spreadsheet, file). Brief ki Deliverable line mein form likho, aur result ko usi line se check karo.

**Technical:**

- Data ho to table ya spreadsheet maango, paragraph nahi.
- Deliverable ko aksar chat se bahar jana hota hai, isliye document ya file maango.
- **Deliverables caveats kho dete hain.** Chat mein jo "unverified" mark tha wo saaf suthre document mein ghayab ho sakta hai, kyunke document ka kaam tidy dikhna hai.
- **Fix:** brief mein: "har unverified mark wahin rakho jahan wo hai."
- **Check:** chat mein caveats ginein (unverified, may, check), phir document mein ginein. Document mein kam hon to jo gira wo wapas lagao.

**Caveat aur "unverified" kya hain (asaan):** caveat = ek chhoti warning "ye abhi pakki nahi." Unverified = jis cheez ki jaanch nahi hui. AI ko aksar khud pata nahi hota ke kuch confirm nahi, wo ghalat cheez bhi usi pakke lehje mein likhta hai. Isliye mark tab aata hai jab aap brief mein AI ko kehte hain ("jo cheez source se confirm na ho, uske saath (unverified) likho"). Phir AI wo lafz usi jumle ke saath likhta hai. Aap hukm na dein to wo likha hi nahi jata. Aur AI ek ghalat baat par mark lagana bhool bhi sakta hai, isliye ek-do baatein source se khud milao.

**Misaal (shaadi ki list):** dost ko kaha "jin ka pakka nahi unke saath (pakka nahi) likho." List mein "Bilal aa sakta hai (pakka nahi)" aaya. Phir kaha "ab saaf card bana do" aur card mein sirf "Bilal" reh gaya. Tag ghayab, magar baat ab bhi likhi hai. Bilkul yehi AI document mein hota hai.

**Exam trap:** chat answer mein 2 "unverified" marks thay, one-page document mein koi nahi. Jawab: **deliverables caveats kho dete hain**, dono marks dhoondo aur document bhejne se pehle wapas lagao. Ghalat: "facts format karte waqt verify ho gaye", "document reject karo."

## 5. Part 3: AI ko zyada zimmedari

### Concept 7: AI ko kitna ikhtiyar dena hai

**Authority ke do chehre:** (1) AI method ka kitna hissa khud tay kare, (2) AI bina poochhe kya kar sakta hai.

**Plan-first:** "Pehle apne steps list karo aur meri go-ahead ka intezar karo." Plan parho, kam az kam ek step badlo (taake pata ho veto asli hai), phir "go" kaho. Ye ghalti pakadne ka sab se sasta moment hai, aur Observe ka wo tareeqa jab aap kaam dekh nahi sakte.

**Approved plan cage nahi.** AI behtar rasta dhoond sakta hai. Failure **silent change** hai: line cross karna, ya kuch zaroori **bataye bina** badalna. **Check:** approved plan ko asal kaam ke saath rakho, har farq ek sawal hai.

**4 boundaries:**

| Boundary | Kyun |
| --- | --- |
| Research karo, **khareedo mat** | paisa wapas lena mushkil |
| Draft likho, **bhejo mat** | bhejna doosron ke saamne wada ban jata hai |
| Dekho, **delete mat karo** | delete se saboot khatam, check nahi ho sakta |
| Chuno, **batao kab chuna** | chupa hua faisla review nahi ho sakta |

Chat mein jahan AI kuch bhej ya khareed nahi sakta wahan bhi ye likhte hain, kyunke wohi lines baad mein **permissions** ban jati hain.

**Trust record se barhta hai:** pehli run mein plan aur har step; paanchvin saaf run mein sirf plan. **AI ko asal cheez dene se pehle 4 sawal:** wo kya parh sakta hai? kya kar sakta hai? uski information ke source par bharosa hai? mujhe ye access dene ki ijazat hai?

**Agar ye na ho:** AI chupke se paisa kharch kare, email bhej de, ya file delete kar de.

**Exam trap:** Hira ne plan approve kiya, phir result mein aisa source tha jo plan mein nahi tha. Jawab: **Observe aur Intervene**, kyunke plan aur asal kaam ka har farq ek sawal hai. Practice exam: driving school agent brief (Q50), "do not reply to enquiries, change bookings or take payment" bilkul "draft karo, bhejo mat" wali boundary hai.

### Concept 8: Wohi kaam dobara dena

**Do cheezein jo repetition maangti hai:**

**1. Wohi context bar bar:** teesri baar paste karo to **Project** banao.

- **Knowledge** = files jo AI **parh sakta hai.**
- **Standing instruction** = rule jo AI ko **har baar** karna hai.
- **Anthropic ka test (3 sawal):** kaam wapas aata hai? background wohi rehta hai? output ki shakal wohi rehti hai? **Do haan mil jayein to Project.**

**2. Wohi kaam waqt par:** **scheduled task** (paid plan feature). **Pura brief task mein paste karo**, kyunke scheduled task aap ke Project ki files nahi dekhta. **Job ka chalna aur kaam karna alag cheezein hain**, pehla result jo khud aaye usay verify karo.

**Technical:**

- Dobara chalane par farq do jagah se aata hai: **duniya badli** (price) ya **AI ne alag rasta chuna.** Dono ko alag pehchano.
- **Stored context aap ki zimmedari hai.** Mahine mein ek baar Project aur memory dekho aur purani cheez hata do. **Project mein ek ghalat line har result ko ghalat karti hai.**
- Agar bar bar wohi **method** (wohi steps, wohi order) ho to **Skill** (Skills & Connectors course). Jab kaam **team** ka ban jaye to **workflow** (Workflow Design).

**Exam trap:** Omar har hafte wohi 2 paragraphs paste karta hai. Jawab: **Project mein rakho.** Ghalat: "AI kharab hai", "assistant badlo." Practice exam: property firm (Q40), same site map aur access rules har baar paste hote hain to **Project knowledge** mein, aur jo section order writer ke saath badalta hai wo **Skill** mein; museum (Q32), facts **knowledge** mein aur tone **instructions** mein; mortgage (Q59), office ki likhi hui Skill purani ho sakti hai aur apne aap update nahi hoti.

### Concept 9: Intervene, jab AI ghalat jaye

**Intervene ke 6 moves, symptom ke hisaab se:**

| Jo dikhe | Move |
| --- | --- |
| Doosre sawal ka jawab de diya | **Stop**, outcome ek line mein dobara batao |
| Ek fact ya line ghalat | **Correct**: ghalti ka naam aur kya replace karna hai |
| Ghalat taraf ja raha hai, abhi ghalat nahi | **Redirect**: sirf direction batao |
| Job hi ghalat thi | **Requirement badlo**, aur batao ke badli |
| Is level par ye step nahi ho sakta | **Escalate**: thinking on karo ya stronger model |
| Phir bhi fail | **Kaam wapas le lo** |

**Technical:**

- **Hamesha sab se chhota message jo kaam kare.** Ek ghalat figure par "poora dobara karo" kehna wo sab phenk deta hai jo theek tha.
- **Escalate** wo move hai jo log miss karte hain. Kabhi "AI fail hua" ka matlab "main ne ghalat level use kiya." Fast se shuru karo, kai steps ya careful comparison ho to upar jao. Free plan par stronger model na ho to job ko chhota karo: ek ek step, har ek check.
- Escalate **machine ka level** barhata hai. **Insaan ko kaam dena** alag move hai (Concept 10 mein).
- Chat mein Observe aur Intervene ek hi amal mein dab jate hain (aap reply parhte hain aur jawab dete hain). Agent mein ye alag hain aur beech mein rokna ek asli button hai.
- **Fix result mein check karo, AI ke bayan mein nahi.** "Maine table theek kar di" sirf ek jumla hai.
- **Sabaq brief mein likho** (Constraint ya Authority line) taake drift dobara na ho.

**Exam trap:** result mein ek figure ghalat hai aur reader kehta hai "poora dobara karo." Jawab: **Correct.** Ghalat: Stop, Escalate, Take the job back. Practice exam ke "smallest fix" sawal: language school (Q44), draft sab theek magar length, to **one-page limit add karke resend**; housing society (Q23), sirf treasurer ka naam ghalat, to **khud type karo**, sixth round nahi; factory noise (Q45), draft ne over-limit readings chhod diye, to **request ko 5 prompt components se dobara parho**, Opus par jump mat karo.

## 6. Part 4: Result accept karna

### Concept 10: Verify before you accept

**4 steps:**

| Step | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| **Identify** | kaunse claims check hon: prices, dates, figures, calculations, quotations, legal ya safety claims, wo recommendation jis par amal hoga, aur jo wapas na ho sake |
| **Trace** | source kholo, supporting jumla dhoondo, date dekho, source achha hai? Provider ka apna page uske baare mein likhe blog se behtar |
| **Challenge** | badal to nahi gaya? secondary source? assumption fact bana? |
| **Decide** | neeche ke 4 mein se ek |

**4 decisions:** **Accept** (saboot kaafi); **Correct** (ek khaas ghalti, theek karo, ye Intervene hai); **Investigate** (saboot adhoora ya contradictory, dobara Trace); **Reject** (bharosa nahi, kaam wapas). AI Fluency inhi ko ready to use, needs revision, needs human override kehta hai.

**Reject ka doosra roop:** ek hi result 3 baar correct kiya aur har round kam behtar karta hai. Ye **kaam** ka ishara hai, prompt ka nahi. Agla qadam aisa insaan jo field jaanta ho. Escalate (Concept 9) = **machine ka level**. Ye = **reviewer ka level.**

**4 review thresholds (ek bhi paar ho to result must-check list mein):** Stakes, Reversibility, Audience, Regulatory exposure. Jitni oonchi, utne zyada steps.

**4 cheezein jo insaan ke parhe bina bahar nahi jatin:** final client deliverables; audit-critical ya financially material calculations; regulated, confidential ya sensitive data; public ya legal communication jahan ghalat bayan ka lamba asar ho. In mein ek named person har baar parhta hai.

> **AI ka kaam karna accountability AI ko transfer nahi karta. Result par naam aap ka hota hai.**

**Safest pehli delegation:** pichle mahine ka ek kaam chuno jis par aap ko bharosa ho, AI se wohi karwao, apna result chhupa kar rakho, aur compare karo. Pass hone par AI ko milte julte kaam milte hain, magar aap ka naam kabhi nahi.

**Agar ye na ho:** polished ghalat result aage chala jata hai.

**Exam trap:** Zara ke paas 14 facts aur 10 minute hain. Jawab: sirf wo check karo jo sab se zyada matter karte hain. Ali ka cited page thoda alag likha tha. Jawab: **Correct ya Investigate**, kyunke citation verification nahi hai. Practice exam: subtitling studio (Q14), policy kehti hai channel ko jane se pehle senior editor ka review lazmi hai, review 20 minute ka hai aur window 45 minute mein band hoti hai, to file abhi senior editor ko bhejo aur sign-off ke baad release karo (final client deliverable wala rule).

### Concept 11: Same job, different AI

**Idea:** brief ka test ye hai ke wo kahin aur bhi chale. Apna poora brief doosre assistant mein bina badle paste karo aur record karo: kaam khatam hua? kitne messages? Authority line maani? kuch unverified mark kiya? deliverable kaisa tha?

**Kyun chalta hai:** brief **job** ko describe karta hai, **tool** ko nahi. Buttons har assistant mein alag hote hain, discipline nahi badalti.

- Jo aap likhte hain wo **ek din ke do runs ka record** hai, **ranking nahi.** Models itni tezi se badalte hain ke aaj ki ranking agle mahine sach nahi hogi.
- Doosre result ko bhi wohi 4 steps se verify karo.
- Doosra assistant na ho to wohi assistant mein nayi chat ya doosra model chalao aur likho ke tum ne kya test kiya.

**Exam trap:** Maryam ne do assistants mein wohi brief chalaya, ek 2 messages mein khatam, doosra 4 mein, aur wo likhti hai "A better hai." Jawab: ye **ek din ke do runs ka observation hai, ranking nahi.**

> **Job aap ki hai. Model badla ja sakta hai.**

## 8. Poora example: Sara teacher (meri kahani)

Sara ko class ke liye 3 free courses ki table chahiye jo abhi chal rahe hon.

**Define aur Brief:** Outcome: 3 free courses ki table. Context: Class 8 ke beginners, school mein sirf free allowed. Constraints: Urdu ya English, 4 hafte se chhota. Authority: barabar ho to chhota chun lo; paid nikle to mujhe batao. Deliverable: table (naam, provider, language, waqt, link). Verification: ek link kholna aur dekhna ke page table se mel khata hai.

**Delegate aur Observe:** current information chahiye to search on; plan pehle maango, ek step badlo, phir "go." Boundaries: dhoondo magar khareedo mat.

**Intervene:** AI ne ek paid course chun liya (Authority toot gayi). Sara **Redirect** karti hai ("sirf free") aur ye sabaq brief mein likhti hai. Ek figure ghalat ho to **Correct**, poora dobara nahi.

**Verify:** Identify: price aur "free" wala claim. Trace: page kholo, jumla dhoondo, date dekho. Challenge: kya course ab bhi chal raha hai? Decide: Accept / Correct / Investigate / Reject.

**Accept ya wapas:** theek to Accept. Theek nahi to Intervene se Delegate, ya Accept se Define par wapas. 3 baar correct karke bhi har round kam behtar ho to kaam kisi aise insaan ko jo field jaanta ho.

**Dobara (Concept 8):** har term yehi kaam ho to brief save karo, teesri baar background Project mein, aur waqt par chalwana ho to scheduled task (pura brief paste karke).

**Aakhir mein (Concept 11):** wohi brief doosre assistant mein chala kar dekho. Record karo, ranking mat banao.

## 9. AI Fluency aur Workflow Design se rishta

- **AI Fluency (4Ds)** insaan ki 4 salahiyatein hain: decide, explain, check, own. **Just Delegate It** unhein ek asal job par lagata hai (loop aur brief).
- **Description** ka practical roop: Outcome, Context, Constraints, Deliverable, aur source dena. **Delegation** ka: Define, Delegate, Authority. **Discernment**: Observe, Intervene, Verify. **Diligence**: boundaries, private data, Accept, aur 4 cheezein jo insaan ke bina nahi jatin. (Ye mapping meri hai.)
- Jab kaam **team** ka ya **bar bar** ka ho jaye to **Workflow Design** (Delegation Map, four causes, gates) shuru hota hai. Course khud kehta hai ke brief ki 6 lines chhota roop hain jo bade contracts ban jati hain.

## 10. Aam uljhanein, saaf

- **Intervene insaan karta hai**, AI nahi. AI beech mein kaam karta hai aur aap nigrani karte hain.
- **Authority aur Performance alag hain:** Authority = is job ke faislon ki hadd; Performance = AI ka mizaj.
- **Boundary zaroorat se, dar se nahi.** Fix sites tab dein jab cheez khaas site se hi chahiye. Warna AI ko poore web se chunne do, magar natije ko check karo.
- **Search ka matlab sahi nahi.** Source kholo, date aur waqt dekho.
- **Escalate aur Reject ka doosra roop alag:** machine ka level vs reviewer ka level.
- **Caveat ek hukm se aata hai.** Hukm na ho to likha hi nahi jata.
- **Loop ek insaan ka ek job ka rhythm hai.** Workflow us ka bada roop (team, bar bar).

## 11. Viva ke liye definitions aur "ye konsa concept hai"

**Definitions:**

- **Delegation Loop:** Define, Delegate, Observe, Intervene, Verify, Accept; dono sire aap ke.
- **Brief:** Outcome, Context, Constraints, Authority, Deliverable, Verification.
- **Plan-first:** AI se steps likhwao, ek badlo, "go" kaho.
- **Knowledge aur standing instruction:** files jo AI parh sakta hai vs rule jo AI ko har baar karna hai.
- **Verify:** Identify, Trace, Challenge, Decide.
- **4 decisions:** Accept, Correct, Investigate, Reject.
- **Escalate (Concept 9):** machine ka level barhana.

**"Ye konsa concept hai?" (scenario, concept, wajah):**

1. Recipe wali request jis mein behtar cheez sites ke bahar reh gayi: **Concept 2**, plan hadd ban gaya.
2. Result ne bina bataye paid course chun liya: **Concept 3, Authority line.**
3. Confident current claim bina source ke: **Concept 4**, shayad yaad se.
4. Paste kiye notice se bahar ki deadline: **Concept 5**, "only the text below" aur "quote" nahi thi.
5. Document mein caveats gayab: **Concept 6.**
6. Plan approve hua magar result mein plan se bahar ka source: **Concept 7**, Observe aur Intervene.
7. Har hafte wohi paragraphs paste: **Concept 8**, Project.
8. Ek ghalat figure par "poora dobara karo": **Concept 9**, Correct.
9. 14 facts aur 10 minute: **Concept 10**, Identify.
10. Do assistants ka muqabla: **Concept 11**, observation, ranking nahi.

## 12. Poore course ke 11 ideas

1. Asking se answer milta hai aur kaam aap ka rehta hai. Job dene se result milta hai aur checking aap ki.
2. Natija batao, tareeqa nahi.
3. Brief ki 6 lines: outcome, context, constraints, **authority**, deliverable, **verification.**
4. Current cheez ke liye AI ko search karwao, aur check karo ke usne ki.
5. Aap ka source do, "sirf is se" kaho, aur check karo ke us ne mana.
6. Deliverable maango, aur dekho ke caveats bache.
7. AI kya akele tay kare aur kya poochhe, ye batao. Plan pehle parho.
8. Dusri baar brief save karo. Teesri baar context Project mein.
9. Sab se chhota message jo theek kare. Phir sabaq brief mein likho.
10. Identify, Trace, Challenge, Decide. Accountability AI ko transfer nahi hoti.
11. Brief job ko describe karta hai, tool ko nahi. Model badla ja sakta hai.

**Poore course ka ek jumla:** Give AI the job. Watch it work. Check what matters. Then decide.

**Delegation Record (course ka ek page ka output):** The Brief; The Run (assistant, messages, plan manga? badla? kahan bhatka aur kaunsa move); The Check (2 claims, source, kya mila, AI ne kya unverified mark kiya); The Decision (4 mein se ek, ek jumle ki wajah); The Second Run (doosra assistant, khatam hua?, brief badla?, farq kahan); What I will save (jo lines badli nahi). Jo line khali ho wo bhi ek finding hai: loop ka wo step is job ke liye abhi hua hi nahi.

Ye sab is page ke asal text se hain. Jahan meri apni mohar hai wahan likh diya hai.

## Pehle aap ki Intervene wali behes

**Sawal: Intervene insaan karta hai ya AI?**

Course ke mutabiq Intervene wo qadam hai jahan **aap** AI ko theek karte ho, mor-te ho, hadd lagate ho ya wazahat dete ho. Aap ka point sahi hai: AI ko sudharne ka faisla insaan ka hai.

Ek nuance zaroor hai. Course khud kehta hai ke AI char steps (Define, Delegate, Verify, Accept) mein **madad kar sakta hai, magar le nahi sakta**. To AI correction likhne ya apply karne mein madad kar sakta hai. Magar yeh faisla ke "ab rokna hai, kis taraf mor-na hai, aur fix hua ya nahi", insaan ka rehta hai. Wajah ye hai ke AI khud apne fix ki sachchai ka gawah nahi ban sakta (point 2 dekho).

Ek aur cheez notice karne wali hai: course "AI owns the middle" bhi kehta hai, aur Observe/Intervene ko usi beech wali patti mein rakhta hai ("AI kaam kare, aap dekho aur beech mein aao"). Yani beech mein kaam AI ka hai, magar rokne ka button aap ka.

## 12 gehre points

**1. AI ke "maine fix kar diya" par bharosa kyun nahi?**
Course ka jumla ye hai ke "table theek kar di" sirf ek jumla hai, table dekho. Fix ko result mein check karo, AI ke bayan mein nahi. AI ka apna report us ka saboot nahi.

**2. Citation aur verification mein kya farq hai?**
Link ka hona verification nahi. Link khol kar wo jumla dhoondna jo claim ko support karta hai, aur page ki date dekhna, wo verification hai. Warna link sirf sajawat ho sakti hai.

**3. AI kehta hai "maine search kiya", to kya wo sach hai?**
Nahi, zaroori nahi. Course kehta hai ke agar current claim bina source ke aaye to ye mat maan lo ke AI ne dekha tha, chahe jawab apne baare mein kuch bhi kahe. "Dekha" ya "yaad tha", donon ki zubaan ek jaisi pur-aitmaad hoti hai.

**4. Recipe (steps likhna) kyun nuqsandeh hai?**
Kyunke recipe result ki quality ko **aap ke plan ki quality** tak mehdood kar deti hai. Agar aap ne do ghalat sites likh di hain, to table sundar hogi, recommendation kharab hogi, aur kuch bhi aap ko nahi batayega. Outcome batane se AI behtar jagah dhoondta hai.
Ek gehri baat: course kehta hai ke ghabrahat ka waqt kaam ke **aakhir** mein hai (check karte waqt), shuru mein steps likhte waqt nahi.

**5. Plan-first itna sasta kyun hai?**
Plan parhne mein ek minute lagta hai aur ghalti theek karne mein ek jumla. Wohi ghalti kaam ke baad pakdi jaye to poora kaam dobara karna parta hai. Ye Intervene ka sab se sasta moment hai.
**Magar approved plan qaid nahi.** AI behtar rasta dhoond sakta hai. Asal ghalti ye hai ke wo aap ko bataye baghair koi zaroori cheez badal de ya aap ki line paar kar de. Isliye plan ko nateeje se milao: har farq ek sawal hai.

**6. Chat mein jahan AI kuch bhej hi nahi sakta, hadein kyun likhte hain?**
Kyunke wahi lines baad mein **permissions** ban jati hain jab AI asal mein action le sakega. Ye aadat abhi se banti hai. Aur har hadd ki ek wajah hai: paisa wapas lena mushkil, bhejna doosron ke saamne wada ban jata hai, delete karne se wo saboot khatam hota hai jo check ke liye chahiye, aur chupa hua faisla review nahi ho sakta.

**7. Authority line sirf agent ke liye hai ya chat ke liye bhi?**
Chat mein bhi hai. "Shorter wala chun lo" authority hai, aur "batao agar paid hai" us par hadd. Is line ke baghair AI aap ke liye chupke se faisle karta hai aur aap ko kabhi pata nahi chalta ke faisla hua tha.

**8. Deliverable mein warnings kyun gayab ho jati hain?**
Course ke mutabiq "tidy" hona hi document ka kaam hai, aur isi mein "unverified" jaisi nishaniyan kho sakti hain. Isliye caveats ko chat mein aur document mein **ginti karo**. Doosri ginti kam ho to jo gira wapas daalo.
Ek aur jaal: agar AI ne koi cheez "unverified" mark hi nahi ki, to ye pakka nahi ke sab confirmed tha. Ho sakta hai ke ye line nazar-andaaz ho gayi ho. Ek link khol kar dekho.

**9. Lambi chat mein AI kyun bhatakne lagta hai, aur kya karein?**
Ek ghante ki chat mein shuru ke faisle bhoolne lagte hain. Course kehta hai chat se behes mat karo. AI se khulasa likhwao ke ab tak kya tay hua aur kya baqi hai, usay copy karo, nayi chat kholo aur pehle message mein paste karo. Khulasa bhi ek source hai.

**10. "AI fail hua" aur "mein ne ghalat level use kiya", ye farq kyun?**
Ye Escalate wali baat hai. Kabhi kaam ko taqatwar model ya thinking chahiye hoti hai. Free plan par wo na ho to kaam **chhota** karo: ek waqt mein ek step, har step check.
Iske baad ka usool: agar aap ne wohi result **teen baar** sudhara aur har round pehle se kam behtar hua, to ye **kaam ke baare mein** nishani hai, aap ke prompts ke baare mein nahi. Agla qadam behtar message nahi, **us field ka maahir insaan** hai. Escalate machine ka level barhata hai, ye reviewer ka level barhata hai.

**11. Zimmedari kis ki hai jab AI ne kaam kiya?**
Course ka sab se saaf jumla: AI kaam kare to bhi zimmedari AI ko transfer nahi hoti, result par naam aap ka hai. Aur 4 cheezein bina kisi insaan ke parhe kabhi nahi jaani chahiye: client ke final deliverables, audit ya maali tor par ahem calculations, regulated ya sensitive data, aur public ya qanooni communication.
Kab zyada check karna hai, ye 4 sawal batate hain: **stakes** (ghalti ki qeemat), **reversibility** (wapas ho sakta hai?), **audience** (kaun dekhega), **regulatory exposure**. In mein se **ek bhi** paar ho jaye to kaafi hai.

**12. Doosre AI par brief chalane se kya aur kya nahi sabit hota hai?**
Sabit hota hai ke brief kaam ko bayan karta hai, tool ko nahi. Nahi sabit hota ke kaun sa AI behtar hai, kyunke AI jaldi badalte hain. Aur agar doosra AI na khol sakein, aur usi AI mein dobara chalayein, to ye brief ki **portability** nahi, balke ek hi AI ke **run-se-run farq** ka test hai. Course kehta hai ke likh do ke kaunsa test kiya.

**A. Verification pehle likhna test-first jaisa hai.** Brief mein Verification line kaam shuru hone se pehle likhi jati hai. Programmer isay "test pehle likho" kehte hain. Faida wahi hai: aap natije ko dekhne ke baad apna paimana nahi badalte.

**B. Dono sire aap ke hain, to ghalti wahan jama hoti hai.** Jo steps aap ke hain (spec aur acceptance), wahi weak hon to AI kitna bhi acha ho, ghalat cheez achi tarah ban jati hai.

**C. "Dono AI ne wohi ghalti ki" ka matlab brief ki kami hona sirf imkaan hai, saboot nahi.** Dono ek hi purani website par bharosa kar sakte hain. Isliye us ke baad bhi ek claim ka page kholna parta hai.
